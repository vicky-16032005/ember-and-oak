# Ember & Oak

A demo marketing website for a fictional wood-fired neighborhood bistro.

- **Register:** brand (design IS the product; single landing page + legal pages)
- **Audience:** design-conscious diners deciding whether to book a table
- **Goal:** one conversion action, "Reserve a table" (mock form, no backend)
- **Voice:** charred, convivial, unhurried; plain sentences, no marketing filler
- **Stack:** React 19 + Vite, Tailwind 3, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Phosphor icons
- **Status:** demo only; reservation form does not transmit data
